Loading MusicXML Files

A guide to accessing your files from cloud storage, your computer, and other apps

This app opens MusicXML files (.xml or .musicxml) stored on your device or in cloud services like Google Drive, iCloud Drive, or Dropbox. Because iOS controls how apps access files, you may need to complete a one-time setup for each cloud service before your files appear.

Option 1: iCloud Drive (Recommended)

iCloud Drive is built into iOS and is the easiest option — no extra app setup required.

First-time setup

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then tap iCloud.
  3. Make sure iCloud Drive is turned on.
  4. Scroll down and confirm the Files app is enabled.

Uploading files from your computer

  1. On a Mac, open Finder and click iCloud Drive in the sidebar.
  2. Drag your .xml or .musicxml files into iCloud Drive (you can create a folder to organize them).
  3. Wait for the files to sync — a cloud icon means they are still uploading.
  4. Once synced, open the app, tap the folder icon, and navigate to iCloud Drive to find your files.
On a Windows PC, install iCloud for Windows from the Microsoft Store, then drag files into the iCloud Drive folder.

Option 2: Google Drive

Google Drive works through iOS's built-in Files app. You will need to connect it once before it appears in the file picker.

First-time setup

  1. Install the Google Drive app from the App Store if you don't already have it.
  2. Open the Google Drive app and sign in to your Google account.
  3. Open the iOS Files app.
  4. Tap Browse at the bottom of the screen.
  5. Tap Edit in the top-right corner.
  6. Toggle on Google Drive and tap Done.

Uploading files from your computer

  1. Go to drive.google.com in a web browser.
  2. Click New → File Upload and select your .xml or .musicxml files.
  3. Wait for the upload to complete.
  4. On your iPhone, open the app and tap the folder icon. Navigate to Google Drive in the file picker.

Troubleshooting: files won't load

If you can see your files in the picker but they won't open, the files may not be downloaded to your device yet.

  1. Open the Google Drive app.
  2. Find the file you want to use.
  3. Tap the three-dot menu (…) next to the file.
  4. Tap Make available offline.
  5. Wait for the download to finish, then try opening the file in the app again.
If Google Drive does not appear in the file picker after setup, try closing and reopening the app.

Option 3: Dropbox

Dropbox also integrates with the iOS Files app and follows the same setup process as Google Drive.

First-time setup

  1. Install the Dropbox app from the App Store.
  2. Open Dropbox and sign in.
  3. Open the iOS Files app, tap Browse, then tap Edit.
  4. Toggle on Dropbox and tap Done.
  5. Upload your .xml or .musicxml files to Dropbox from your computer, then access them through the app's file picker.

Other ways to get files onto your device

Email attachment

Email yourself a .xml or .musicxml file. On your iPhone, tap and hold the attachment, then tap Save to Files.

AirDrop from a Mac

Right-click a file on your Mac and choose Share → AirDrop. Select your iPhone, then choose Save to Files when it arrives.

USB transfer (Mac)

Connect your iPhone via USB. Open Finder, click your iPhone in the sidebar, select the Files tab, and drag files into the app's folder.
