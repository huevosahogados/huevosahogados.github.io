# Interleaves — User Guide

*A practice tool for musicians: import your sheet music, mark out the trouble spots, and practice them in randomized, timed rotation.*

---

## The Big Picture

Interleaves works with two kinds of objects:

- **Scores** are your source documents — a PDF or photo of sheet music, all pages together. You can browse and practice a Score directly, page by page, right after importing it. No setup required.
- **Passages** are the trouble spots you circle out of a Score for focused, shuffled practice. A Passage always belongs to one Score.

You don't have to prep everything before you start playing. Import a piece, start practicing it whole, and mark passages as you discover them — whenever you notice a trouble spot.

---

## 1 · Import

Tap **+ Import**. A window opens and stays open — add files or take photos, and keep adding without it closing on you.

- **Naming a piece** before your first photo auto-labels every page that follows ("pg.2", "pg.3"...). You can also skip naming and rename later in the sidebar.
- **PDFs** come in as one Score with every page intact — no need to split anything.
- Tap **Import all** when you're done adding.

---

## 2 · Practicing a Score directly

Every imported Score shows up in the **Scores** section of the sidebar. Click to expand it and see its pages. Click a page to view it. You can hit **▶ practice** any time — Scores without passages yet get shuffled in page by page.

---

## 3 · Saving a Passage

While viewing a Score page:

1. Circle or box the tricky bit.
2. Tap **Save passage**.
3. Give it a name (a suggestion is pre-filled).
4. If the passage spans two pages, check **"Continues on next page"** and use the page controls that appear.

Once a Score has at least one saved Passage, it's automatically removed from the practice shuffle — the idea being that once you're working a piece via Passages, you probably don't want the whole thing popping up randomly too. If you *do* still want the whole piece in rotation, there's a checkbox next to the Score's name in the sidebar to toggle it back in.

---

## 4 · Score Edit — permanent notes

Tap **✎ Score Edit** on any Score page for things that should stick around: fingerings, corrections, bowings.

This opens a separate full-screen editor. Nothing is saved until you tap **Save** there — tap **Cancel** to discard. Once saved, these marks automatically appear in every Passage made from that page — you never have to re-mark them.

Score Edit also has its own **Clarify** tool, which sharpens a photo (useful for camera shots and e-ink readability).

---

## 5 · Practice Sessions

Tap **▶ practice**. Passages (and any Scores still in the pool) shuffle with a timer per page.

- **− less / + more** and the tier dot (·, ↑, ↓) in the sidebar control how often something comes up in the shuffle.
- **Weight** (×1–×3) gives an item more time per turn without changing how often it appears.
- **Pause** to draw practice marks on whatever's currently showing — these are local to that one Passage.
- **Go to score** (while paused) jumps out to the source Score so you can circle a brand-new passage. Nothing about your current session is lost — tap **▶ Continue practicing** in the topbar to pick back up exactly where you left off.

---

## Compás (the metronome)

Tap **BPM** to open Compás.

- Drag the dial or type a tempo directly.
- Tap a beat-count button (2–7) to set the meter, or tap the active one again for a free click with no bar structure.
- Click beat **"1"** to cycle its sound: downbeat chime → accent → plain.
- **Tap** button sets tempo by tapping along.

**Saving tempo to a Passage:** whenever a Passage is open — in the editor, or as the current item mid-practice — a **"Save Compás"** button appears. It captures tempo, meter, accents, beat-1 mode, and the panel's on-screen position, all tied to that one Passage. Next time that Passage comes up, Compás configures itself automatically (you still have to hit Start yourself).

---

## Rest Periods

On by default. Every few practice items, you get a short break with a suggested stretch, breathing exercise, or eye rest. Adjust the frequency, duration, or turn suggestions off entirely in **Settings**.

---

## Handy to Know

- **Undo / redo** — Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z work while drawing anywhere in the app (Score Edit, a Passage, or mid-practice). Capped at 20 steps, and cleared whenever you switch to a different page or passage.
- **Themes** — Ocean, Warm, E-ink, B&W, and Clean White are available in Settings, including an e-ink mode tuned for low-refresh tablets.
- **Backup** — export your whole library (Scores and Passages) as a single file from Settings, and import it back on another device or after a reset.
- **Manual reordering** — drag the ⠿ handle next to any Passage in the sidebar to change its position for manual-order sessions.
