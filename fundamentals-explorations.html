7 to 8pm

Fundamentals

The same hour every week, and it changes you slowly. We warm up, work on time and tone, and practice changing chords without a hitch. A lot of it is listening: hearing where the bass goes before your hand gets there.

Beginners and working players do the same exercises. Each person gets something different out of them, and nobody is behind.

8 to 9pm

Explorations

One topic at a time, for as long as it needs. Some fit in a single night. Others take four or eight weeks. Series build week to week, so they're sold as a set.
