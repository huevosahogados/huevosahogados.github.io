Rooted in music.
Built for musicians.

Stuart Zobel has spent his career at the intersection of music-making and the tools that support it. Whether teaching a student to read a lead sheet, performing with Cafecito, or writing the code that lets a saxophonist transpose on the fly, his work shares a common thread: making music more accessible, more portable, and more alive.
